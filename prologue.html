Prologue — Scene 0 (Mother's voice)

"I don't know how big your world will become,
but I want you to have something that can grow with you."

"This little bear… it's my first birthday gift for you.
I hope one day, when you look at it,
it will remind you of where you began."

She places something soft and warm into your tiny hands.
You cannot speak yet, but you feel her voice like a quiet light.

"And this… is how my story began."

Prologue — Scene 0.5 (Bear's monologue)

"I was given to you before you knew the world.
Before your first words, before your first memories—
I was already there."

"I don't have a heartbeat or breath,
but I remember the warmth of that day."

"I wasn't just a gift.
I was meant to keep the pieces of your story safe…
even the ones you might forget."

"When you open your eyes again, I will be waiting."
